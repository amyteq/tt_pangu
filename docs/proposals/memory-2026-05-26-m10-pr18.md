The `docs/milestones/README.md` M10 section already captures the substantive scope decisions confirmed by PR #18 (competition, single-push policy, sequencing, worktree). The novel item PR #18 introduces is a **process convention** (per-spec `### Related issues` sections). That belongs in `PROJECT_STATE.md` "Current milestone" because:

- It is M10-specific until the open question above is answered.
- It is the kind of fact a future agent reading PROJECT_STATE first needs to know to follow the local convention when authoring future M10 specs (if any are added) or when reviewing the M10 PR.
- It is not architecture (no ADR needed); it is not a long-lived decision (no handoff needed yet).

If the open question "promote to engineering-practice.md?" resolves "yes", a follow-up proposal can move this bullet out of PROJECT_STATE into engineering-practice and remove the PROJECT_STATE bullet.
