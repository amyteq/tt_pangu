# Rules — Orbit Wars

> The installed engine is ground truth (`kaggle_environments/envs/orbit_wars/orbit_wars.py`); the web page is
> JS-rendered and unreadable by fetchers. Full mechanics + engine line refs are in [`README.md`](./README.md)
> ("Ground truth"). Facts with evidence; uncertain items marked **⚠ unverified**.

## Problem
Agent-vs-agent real-time strategy: over 500 steps on the `kaggle_environments` orbit_wars engine, capture and
hold planets to win. Engine supports 2-player and 4-player; the **scored ladder is 1v1**.

## Data
- No static dataset; games are generated by the engine (self-play + public-agent suite).
- A public episode dataset exists for replay mining: `kaggle/orbit-wars-episodes-2026-06-08` (~1.28 GB).

## Metric
- **skill-rating (μ) ladder**, μ0=600, **1v1**; win/loss drives a **pairwise μ update** (winner μ↑, loser μ↓,
  moved toward their mean). Margin does not matter — only win/loss.

## Submission format
- A **single-file agent** (`stdlib` + `kaggle_environments`) or a tar.gz package (e.g. adapted kuni05 with
  `orbit_lite/` + torch). Per-turn budget **1000 ms** (ours ~4 ms; crash-safe wrapper returns `[]` on error).
- **torch is usable on the eval side** (empirically: torch kuni05 scored ~1137/1151).

## Constraints
- **5 submissions/day**; the competition keeps only your **latest 2** active on the ladder.
- Engine is pure-Python, **CPU-bound — GPU does not help**; parallelize across cores.

## Deadline / schedule
- deadline **2026-06-23**; final eval ~2 weeks after. $50,000 Featured; competition id **138420**; ~4137 teams.

## External data / kernel / internet / GPU rules
- Public opponent agents are lifted from public notebooks (attributed); vendored opponents are gitignored and
  rebuilt via `arena/_reconstruct_pkg.py` / `arena/_extract_pub.py`.
- Compliance (issue #87): dual account = real registered teammates (harrysun2006 + firefly2006); same-competition
  second-account submission is **compliance-gated** in `submit.py` (`--i-confirm-compliance`) and needs human
  approval; never print/submit credentials.
